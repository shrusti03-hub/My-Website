# StyleMuse — Fashion Affiliate Website

A complete responsive fashion + affiliate marketing starter website.

## Files
- `index.html` — website structure
- `styles.css` — responsive styling
- `script.js` — mobile menu, product filters, save buttons and newsletter demo

## How to open
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open `index.html` in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.

## Important customizations

### 1. Change the brand
Search for `StyleAura` in `index.html` and replace it with your chosen business name.

### 2. Add your affiliate links
Search for:
`https://example.com`

Replace each one with your real affiliate product URL.

The product links already use:
`rel="nofollow sponsored"`

### 3. Change product names and prices
Edit the product card text inside the "SHOP THE LOOK" section.

### 4. Change Pinterest link
Replace:
`https://www.pinterest.com/`

with your Pinterest Business profile URL.

### 5. Change contact email
Replace:
`hello@stylemuse.com`

with your real business email.

### 6. Images
This starter uses remote Unsplash images. Internet is needed for them to appear.
You can later replace them with your own product/fashion images.

### 7. Newsletter
The newsletter form is currently a front-end demo. For real email collection, connect it to a provider such as Mailchimp, Brevo, ConvertKit, or your backend.

## Affiliate disclosure
A basic affiliate disclosure is already included. Before publishing commercially, add complete Privacy Policy, Terms, Cookie Policy (where required), and any disclosures required by your affiliate programs and local laws.

## Publishing
This static site can be deployed on platforms such as:
- GitHub Pages
- Netlify
